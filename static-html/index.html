About the Doctor

A dedicated partner for your brain & spine health

Dr. Khalid Khan Zadran is a Consultant Neurosurgeon and Brain & Spine Surgeon serving patients at Rifah Medical Complex in Kabul. He is recognized as the first FCPS neurosurgeon in Afghanistan — a milestone in the country's medical history.

He completed his medical education at Ayub Medical College in Abbottabad and is certified by the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan (CPSP), earning the Fellowship (FCPS) that represents advanced specialist training in surgery. Alongside his clinical practice, Dr. Zadran works as a researcher studying brain disorders affecting patients in Afghanistan.

Common Questions, Clear Answers

Helpful information about consultations, conditions and the technology used in treatment.

Dr. Zadran treats brain conditions including tumors, head trauma and structural brain disorders; spinal problems such as disc disease, nerve compression and cases requiring complex reconstruction; and peripheral nerve conditions requiring diagnosis and surgical treatment.

Neuronavigation is an image-guided surgical system — like a GPS for the operating room. Detailed scans of the patient's anatomy are mapped so the surgeon can plan a precise approach to the target area, helping protect healthy brain and spinal tissue during the operation.
